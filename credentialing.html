Questions about provider credentialing and payer enrollment.

How long does credentialing typically take?

Timelines vary by payer, but our team typically completes enrollment in 60–90 days versus 120+ days industry average.

Yes. We create, maintain, and update CAQH profiles as part of our credentialing service.

We support multi-state credentialing and licensing for providers practicing across state lines.

Yes. We proactively manage re-credentialing and renewal deadlines so your billing is never interrupted.
