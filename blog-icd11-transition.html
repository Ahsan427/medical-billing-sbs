ICD-11 Transition: What Practices Need to Know

The transition from ICD-10 to ICD-11 represents the most significant coding change in over a decade. While the U.S. adoption timeline is still evolving, practices that prepare early will avoid disruption and revenue loss.

What's Changing

ICD-11 introduces new code structures, expanded detail for mental health and musculoskeletal conditions, and improved alignment with SNOMED CT terminology. The code format and lookup systems will differ from ICD-10-CM.

Timeline Expectations

The WHO released ICD-11 in 2019, but U.S. implementation requires CMS approval and mapping. Current estimates suggest a transition window in the late 2020s, but practices should begin planning now.

How to Prepare Your Team

  • Invest in coder training and ICD-11 certification programs
  • Audit your current documentation practices for gaps
  • Ensure your EHR vendor has an ICD-11 migration roadmap
  • Budget for software updates and staff education
