5 Ways to Reduce Claim Denials in 2026

Claim denials remain one of the biggest revenue drains for medical practices. In 2026, payers continue to tighten requirements, making proactive denial prevention more critical than ever. Here are five proven strategies to reduce your denial rate.

1. Verify Eligibility Before Every Visit

Real-time eligibility verification catches coverage issues before services are rendered. Implement automated checks at scheduling and check-in to avoid preventable denials.

2. Improve Clinical Documentation

Many denials stem from insufficient documentation to support the codes billed. Regular provider education on documentation requirements for common procedures can dramatically reduce coding-related denials.

3. Scrub Claims Before Submission

Use claim scrubbing software to catch errors — missing modifiers, incorrect codes, invalid NPI numbers — before claims reach the payer. This alone can improve your clean claim rate by 15–20%.

4. Track Denial Trends

Categorize denials by reason code and identify patterns. If 30% of your denials share the same root cause, fixing that one issue recovers significant revenue.

5. Appeal Aggressively and On Time

Many denied claims are actually payable upon appeal. Track payer appeal deadlines and maintain templates for common denial scenarios to speed up the recovery process.
