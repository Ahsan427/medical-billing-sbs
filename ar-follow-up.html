FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about accounts receivable follow-up services.

What AR aging do you typically work on?

We follow up on all outstanding balances — 30, 60, 90 days and beyond — for both insurance and patient accounts.

How much can AR follow-up improve collections?

Clients typically see a 20–30% improvement in aged receivables within the first 90 days.

Do you handle patient balance collections?

Yes. We provide professional patient outreach including statements, calls, and payment plan setup.

Weekly AR aging reports and monthly collection summaries keep you informed of all follow-up activity.
